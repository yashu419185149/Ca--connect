About the Department

The department combines strong theoretical foundations with practical skills in programming, database management, software development and system design. Modern areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Mobile App Development, Cybersecurity and Cloud Computing are integrated into learning through projects, coding labs, internships and workshops, with continuous mentoring by experienced faculty.


Vision

To emerge as a renowned center in the field of computer applications for providing high quality education and research to produce globally competent professionals with ethical values.

Mission

  • M1: High quality education with a cutting-edge curriculum and learner-centered approach.
  • M2: A culture of innovation and research through industry-academia linkages.
  • M3: Ethical, socially-conscious professionals with leadership qualities.

What You'll Learn

  • Programming, software development & databases
  • AI, Cloud and Cybersecurity
  • Analytical thinking & problem-solving
  • Scalable applications for industry needs

Programs Offered

BCA

Undergraduate program in Computer Applications, aligned with NEP 2020.

MCA

Postgraduate program with industry-relevant courses in AI, Data Science, Cloud and Cybersecurity.

Ph.D.

Doctoral research in Computer Applications with emphasis on innovation-driven research.


Teaching-learning: Project-based learning with industry partners, flipped classrooms with real-time problem-solving sessions, and adaptive learning with research integration and skill development.
